Getting Started

1.1 What Is Oobeo Permits?

Oobeo Permits v2 is a web-based permit management system for parking. It lets operators create, sell, and manage parking permits across one or many locations. It handles applications, billing, payments, vehicle tracking, and reporting in one platform.

Permits v2 runs on the Oobeo platform alongside Valet, Self Parking, Attendant Payments, and Event Parking. All products share a common database, so driver records, payment data, and reporting are consolidated.

The system is designed around three user types:

RoleWho They AreWhat They Do
CustomerParkers who need a permitFind permits, apply, pay, manage vehicles, view invoices
EmployeeProperty managers, CS reps, front deskCreate permits, manage accounts, process payments, review applications
AdministratorOperations directors, IT adminsSet up locations, configure rates, manage users, build share links

1.2 How to Access the System

Employees and Administrators

Access the Permits dashboard at vale.to/controlpanel/permits. Use Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge. No installation required.

The Permits v2 application (React frontend) has its own sidebar navigation separate from the legacy Oobeo dashboard. Both are accessible, and you can switch between them using the Quick Actions panel in the top-right corner.

The Permits v2 dashboard showing the Accounts tab, summary bar, and sidebar navigation
Quick Actions panel: switch between Permits, Dashboard (legacy), and EnforcePlus. Download links for Oobeo and EnforcePlus mobile apps.

Customers

Customers do not use the admin dashboard. They interact through:

  • Share links sent by email, text, or posted on signage
  • QR codes on signage, in lobbies, or onboarding materials
  • Parking Finder, a public search page showing locations with available permits
  • Account Management Portal, a self-serve page for payments, receipts, and permit management

No app download required. The entire customer experience is mobile web.

The Parking Finder: customers search for available permits by location

1.3 Key Concepts

AccountThe billing entity. An individual or organization. Gets charged for permits. Has its own portal link.
Permit HolderThe person using the permit. Same as Account owner for personal permits, or different for corporate accounts.
RateA pricing configuration: cost, interval, capacity, options, and requirements. Created via a 4-step wizard.
LocationA physical parking facility. Each has its own rates, capacity, and settings.
Share LinkA URL (with optional QR code) for customers to apply. Can be scoped to location, rate, or account.
Management PortalA customer-facing self-serve page for each account and permit. Accessed via unique link.
Part 2

Customer Guide

customer

For parkers who need to apply for, pay for, and manage a parking permit.

2.1 Finding Available Permits

Parking Finder

Your operator may provide a search page where you can browse locations and available permits. Enter a location name or address to find parking near you.

Share Link or QR Code

Links sent by email/text or QR codes on signage open the application form directly, often pre-scoped to a specific location or rate.

Account Portal

If you already have an account, your portal has a "Find Permits" button to search for additional permits at your operator's locations.

The Parking Finder page: search by location to find and apply for available permits

2.2 Applying for a Permit

Once you reach the application portal:

  1. Select a location (if not pre-selected).
  2. Choose a rate. Each shows the price, billing frequency, and any notes from the operator.
  3. Enter your information: name, email, phone, address.
  4. Add your vehicle(s): license plate, color, make, type.
  5. Review and submit.

After submitting, your application goes to the operator for review. Some rates have auto-approval enabled, meaning your permit is activated immediately.

2.3 Your Account Portal

Every account has a self-serve portal where you can manage your permits, make payments, and view receipts. Access it via the link in your confirmation email or invoice.

The Account Management Portal: view balance, make payments, manage permits, and find additional parking

Your portal shows:

  • Your account info and email
  • Permit count and location
  • Balance Due with status alerts (e.g., "Suspended - pay now to avoid action")
  • Three action buttons: View Receipts, Manage Permits, Payment Methods
  • A Pay Now button for immediate payment
  • A Find Permits link to apply for additional permits

2.4 Paying for Your Permit

When you have a balance due, click Pay Now from your portal or from the link in your invoice email. The payment page shows your invoice details and total due.

The payment page: review invoice details and click Pay now to complete your payment
MethodHow It Works
Credit CardPay online through your portal or invoice link.
ACH (Bank Transfer)Pay online via bank transfer.
CashPay in person. The operator records payment in the system.
CheckMail to the address on your invoice. Recorded by operator when received.

Use the Payment Methods button in your portal to manage saved payment methods.

2.5 Managing Your Permit

From your portal, click Manage Permits to view your active permits. To change your vehicle or update your information, contact your parking operator. To cancel, contact the operator. Cancellation policies vary.

2.6 Getting Help

For questions about your permit, billing, or changes, contact your parking operator. Their contact information is available via the Contact link on the Parking Finder page and in your invoice emails.

The Permits v2 sidebar has six sections: Permits (the main workspace), Manage, Reports, Communications, Location Settings, and Share Links. Within Permits, five tabs give you access to Accounts, Permits, Rates, Invoices, and Locations.

A location filter at the top scopes all data. When active, a summary bar shows Total Permits, Available, Occupancy %, pending Applications, and Wait List.

Every tab supports Search, customizable Columns, Filters, and Export. Your column and filter selections are saved.

3.3 Creating a Permit

Click + Add Permit in the top-right corner to launch the wizard.

Step 1: Choose Existing account (recommended) or create a New account

Existing Account

Search by name or email. Recent accounts appear automatically. Click the account, then "Continue to permit details."

Search for an existing account by name or email

New Account

A 3-step wizard creates the account: Basic Info (name, phone, email, optional billing email), Addresses (optional), and Settings. The account is created, then you proceed to permit details.

New account wizard: enter account name, phone, email, and optional billing email

Permit Details

After selecting or creating an account, configure the permit: Location, Rate (dropdown), Start Date, Space Number (optional), Access ID (optional), and Cancel on renewal checkbox. Expand the Permit Holder and Vehicles sections or skip them.

The permit details form with collapsible Permit Holder and Vehicles sections

3.4 Managing Accounts

Click any account row to open the detail view. The left sidebar has five sections:

Account Overview: outstanding balance (151 days past due), Take Payment, Add/Subtract Credit, unpaid invoices, and account attributes

Overview

Shows account name, contact info, attributes (e.g., "No late fee", "1 Vehicles per permit"), outstanding balance with days past due, Take Payment and Archive Account buttons, credit balance with Add/Subtract, and unpaid invoices with amounts and periods.

Activity

Full audit trail: every invoice generated, email sent, payment recorded, and change made. Filterable by activity type with a Refresh button.

Activity Log tracking New Invoice and Email Sent events with dates, times, and amounts

Permits

All permits under this account with location, rate, count, frequency, and total. Edit Quantity inline or click + Add Permit to create a new one from this account.

Permits tab on an account: edit quantity inline or add new permits

Management Portal

A unique link (vale.to/checkout/account/...) to the customer-facing portal for this account. Copy and send to the customer so they can self-serve payments, view receipts, and manage permits.

Management Portal with copyable link and Open Portal button

Notes

Free-text notes with attachments. Use for internal tracking (e.g., "Called about late payment 3/15, promised to pay by Friday").

Notes with text input, attachment support, and Save button

3.5 Reviewing Applications

When a customer submits an application through a share link, the summary bar shows the count under "Applications" with a "View" link. The legacy dashboard also shows a yellow alert banner.

Dashboard alert: "You have 4 pending permit applications - Review Pending Permits"

For each application, you can Approve (creates the permit and first invoice), Reject (notifies the customer), or Request more information.

Rates with "Auto Approve Applicants" enabled (configured in the rate wizard, Step 3) skip the review queue and activate permits immediately.

3.6 Processing Payments and Invoices

Online payments (credit card, ACH) are processed automatically when the customer pays through their portal. For offline payments (cash, check), open the invoice and record the payment.

Invoice detail showing Parking Fees and Convenience Fee line items, with PDF, Resend, Recalculate, Refund, and Edit buttons

Invoice actions:

  • PDF: Download a PDF of the invoice
  • Resend: Re-send the invoice to the customer
  • Recalculate: Recalculate the invoice (e.g., after a rate change)
  • Refund: Process a refund on a paid invoice
  • Edit: Modify the invoice details

3.7 Managing Unpaid Invoices

  1. Go to Invoices tab, click Show Unpaid Only.
  2. Review each invoice. Click for details and communication history.
  3. Use Resend to re-send the invoice or Communications to send a payment reminder.
  4. If unresolved, open the permit and use Suspend Permit or one of the cancellation options.

Click any permit row to open the detail view.

Click Edit in the left sidebar to change the location, rate, start date, space number, access ID, or cancel-on-renewal setting. Expand Permit Holder and Vehicles sections to update those as well.

3.9 Communications

Send messages to account holders or permit holders via email or SMS. A two-step wizard: select recipients, then compose your message.

Step 1: Select recipients by Account Holders or Permit Holders. Filter by tags, search, or Select All.

3.10 Tips for Employees

→
Use Search aggressively. Name, account ID, permit number, plate, or phone across most tabs.
→
Send the Management Portal link. Copy the portal link from the account detail and send it to customers. Let them self-serve payments and receipts.
→
Check the audit trail first. Every account and permit has a full Activity Log. Check before escalating.
→
Use notes. When you call a customer, update the account notes. The next person will thank you.
→
Exports respect filters. Set up a filtered view, then Export. You get exactly what's on screen.

4.1 Setting Up Locations

Locations are managed in Location Settings. Each location defines which parking types are enabled (Valet, Self Parking with event parking, Permits), along with address, timezone, tags, and terms and conditions.

Location settings: Parking Options (Valet, Self Parking, Permits), location name, timezone, tags, and address
Locations tab in Permits v2: name, address, occupancy, capacity, and rate count per location

4.2 Configuring Rates

Rates are the core pricing configuration. Click + Create Rate on the Rates tab to launch the 4-step wizard.

Rates tab showing multiple rate types with capacity, occupancy, pricing, interval, schedule, and public/active status

Step 1: Location & Basic Info

Select the location, name the permit type (e.g., "Monthly Unreserved"), set total spaces (capacity), and optionally assign a General Ledger Code for accounting exports.

Rate wizard Step 1: Location, Permit Type name, Total Spaces, General Ledger Code

Step 2: Pricing & Schedule

Set the rate amount, toggle prorations, add a setup fee (one-time charge on first invoice), choose the billing interval (Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly, Seasonal), configure schedule restrictions, and add recurring add-ons.

Pricing & Schedule: rate amount with prorations, setup fee, invoice interval, schedule, and add-ons

Step 3: Options & Requirements

Toggle options that control how this rate behaves:

Step 4: Notes

Private Notes are visible only to staff. Public Notes are visible to customers when they view or apply for this rate. Both support rich text formatting.

Private Notes (staff only) and Public Notes (customer-visible) with rich text editors

4.3 Share Links and Distribution

Share Links (sidebar) is where you create and distribute permit application links. Three sections:

Parking Finder

A public search page (vale.to/app/search-parking/) where customers find locations and apply. Customize with your branding by uploading a header image (PNG/JPG/GIF, up to 2MB). Share the link or embed it on your website.

Parking Finder: embeddable search URL, search type selector, and portal customization with image upload

Send Invites

Generate targeted application links scoped by Location (required), Permit Rate (optional), and Account (optional). Use to invite specific groups to apply for specific permit types.

Configure sharing options: Location, Permit Rate, and Account scoping

Application Form

Same scoping as Send Invites (Location, Rate, Account) but generates a reusable application form link rather than a one-time invite.

Appendix

Frequently Asked Questions

customer employee administrator

General

What browsers are supported?

Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge (latest versions). Fully web-based, no installation.

Account vs. Permit Holder?

Account = billing entity (who pays). Permit Holder = individual using the permit. Same for personal permits, different for corporate.

Customers

How do I pay my balance?

Use the Pay Now button on your Account Management Portal or click the payment link in your invoice email. See Section 2.4.

How do I manage my payment methods?

Click Payment Methods on your Account Management Portal to add, update, or remove saved payment methods.

How do I change my vehicle?

Contact your parking operator. They can update the vehicle on your permit from the admin dashboard.

Employees

How do I cancel a permit?

Open the permit detail. Four options: Suspend Permit, Cancel at Renewal, Cancel on Date, or Cancel Immediately. See Section 3.8.

How do I record a cash or check payment?

Open the invoice, use the Take Payment or record offline payment option. Or open the Account detail and click Take Payment. See Section 3.6.

Can I send a message to all permit holders at once?

Yes. Go to Communications, choose Account Holders or Permit Holders, click Select All, compose your message, and send via email or SMS. See Section 3.9.

Administrators

Why is occupancy over 100%?

Active permits exceed the configured capacity for that location or rate. Review capacity in Location Settings or on the rate configuration.

How do I set up auto-approval for a rate?

In the rate creation wizard, Step 3 (Options & Requirements), toggle on "Auto Approve Applicants." See Section 4.2.
